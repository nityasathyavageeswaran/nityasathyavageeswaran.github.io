We study the trade-off between age of information (AoI) and maximal leakage (MaxL) when an adversary eavesdrops on the server–monitor link to infer the timing of source updates. For Bernoulli updates under two classes of LCFS service policies, coupled (preemptive) and decoupled (dumping), we characterize the policy that minimizes AoI for a given MaxL rate. Decoupled dumping policies give a better age–leakage trade-off, and under a MaxL constraint the optimal dumping strategy dithers between two adjacent deterministic dump periods.

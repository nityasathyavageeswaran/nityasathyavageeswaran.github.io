A shared server must balance clearing a queue of jobs against sending status updates to a remote monitor. We model this as a Markov decision process that minimizes a weighted combination of average age of information and average queue length, show that the optimal scheduling policy is a monotone threshold rule on the queue state whose switching curve changes shape across priority regimes, and compare it with heuristic policies.

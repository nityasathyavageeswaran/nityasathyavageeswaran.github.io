Resource-constrained devices can be augmented with a server-side model, with a rejector deciding which samples to defer to the server. We extend the Learning to Help (L2H) model from binary to multi-class classification, covering settings where server access is limited by cost, availability, or policy, and derive a stage-switching surrogate loss that is differentiable, convex, and consistent with the Bayes rule for the 0-1 loss.
